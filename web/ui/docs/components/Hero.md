- Art: the 3-D oxide lattice (Fe in `oxide-bright`, O in `on-oxide-deep`, bonds as faint `on-oxide-deep` lines) on `oxide-deep` — a still of MarkMotion's open state, or MarkMotion itself. Not screenshots or photos.
