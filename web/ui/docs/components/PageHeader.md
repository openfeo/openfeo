# PageHeader

The top of every panel page: `ofo-pagehead` with optional `__crumbs`, the `__title` (page name, or the object name in mono plus a StatusPill), a `__sub` line of live facts, and `__actions` on the right.

**Provide** the title, one sub line (mode · uptime · last change), and at most one primary action plus one or two secondary.

**Don't** repeat the sidebar label as a crumb on top-level pages — crumbs are for detail pages only.
